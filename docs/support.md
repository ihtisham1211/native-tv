# Support Native Tv

Native Tv is free and MIT-licensed. Donating is optional.

[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/ihtisham12z){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

A donation on [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/ihtisham12z) goes to Ihtisham Arif, the maintainer of this repository. The **Sponsor** button at the top of the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ihtisham1211/native-tv) leads to the same page.

## Other ways to help

* **Star the repository.** [ihtisham1211/native-tv on GitHub](https://github.com/ihtisham1211/native-tv) is easier to find for other owners of rooted LG TVs the more it is starred.
* **Report a TV.** A TV model and webOS version that works, or does not, belongs in [tested TVs](getting-started/tested-tvs.md); [open an issue](https://github.com/ihtisham1211/native-tv/issues) with the details.
* **Contribute.** [Contributing](development/contributing.md) explains how to build, test and open a pull request.
